[h1]ParaTraits: Sims-style traits for your Paras[/h1]
14 new traits you can give any Para, for more individuality. Each one changes what they like to do, how they feel about it, and adds little spontaneous quirks.

[b]Just subscribe, no install steps.[/b] It is a normal content mod.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
Open a Para's personality in the Para creator. The [b]Lifestyles[/b] section is now called [b]Lifestyle & Traits[/b] and has a switch for each new trait. Turn on as many as you like (around 3 feels most Sims-like).

[h2]The traits[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Foodie[/b]: cooks more often, savours meals (Happy), feels inspired cooking, learns Cooking faster.
[*][b]Glutton[/b]: gets hungry faster, snacks for no reason, happy after eating.
[*][b]Gym Rat[/b]: works out often, flexes and does push-ups out of nowhere, post-workout high, trains faster, less TV.
[*][b]Couch Potato[/b]: loves TV and zoning out, finds exercise uncomfortable and avoids it.
[*][b]Lazy[/b]: takes random naps, avoids chores and exercise, hates chores, tires out faster.
[*][b]Music Lover[/b]: puts music on, plays piano and guitar and dances more, happy after music, learns Piano and Guitar faster.
[*][b]Artsy[/b]: paints and doodles more, gets a creative spark (Inspired), learns Painting faster.
[*][b]Techie[/b]: codes, designs, games and scrolls more, gets in the zone, learns Programming faster.
[*][b]Stargazer[/b]: heads to the telescope at night, gets starstruck (Inspired), learns Astronomy faster.
[*][b]Nature Lover[/b]: digs in the dirt, smells the flowers, takes walks, happy after fresh air.
[*][b]Romantic[/b]: daydreams about love, feels flirty after kisses, hugs and slow dances.
[*][b]Hot-Headed[/b]: randomly gets angry now and then, kicks things in frustration.
[*][b]Social Butterfly[/b]: chats more, starts conversations, happy while in company.
[*][b]Loner[/b]: chats less, gives themselves mirror pep talks, uncomfortable in groups.
[/list]
The new feelings show up by name in each Para's emotion breakdown, e.g. "Savoured a meal".

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Traits stack with the built-in Vibe, Social and Talent traits.
[*]Opposite traits (Gym Rat + Couch Potato, Social Butterfly + Loner) can both be on; they will mostly cancel out.
[*]Townies start without these traits; give them out in the Para creator.
[*]Made for Early Access 0.1.9b. Remove it any time by unsubscribing; Paras just lose the extra traits.
[/list]
